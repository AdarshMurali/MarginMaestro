# Credit Support Annex — Henderson Asset Management (CP-4)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Henderson Asset Management is USD 365,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Henderson Asset Management is USD 27,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Henderson Asset Management, with the haircuts shown:

- Cash (USD) (haircut: 0%)
- Investment-grade corporate bonds (haircut: 8%)
- Money market fund shares (haircut: 1%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Henderson Asset Management below BB triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
