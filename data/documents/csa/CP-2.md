# Credit Support Annex — Walker Financial (CP-2)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Walker Financial is USD 95,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Walker Financial is USD 40,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Walker Financial, with the haircuts shown:

- Cash (USD) (haircut: 0%)
- Money market fund shares (haircut: 1%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Walker Financial below BBB- triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
