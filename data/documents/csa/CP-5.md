# Credit Support Annex — Hall Financial (CP-5)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Hall Financial is USD 240,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Hall Financial is USD 21,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Hall Financial, with the haircuts shown:

- Cash (USD) (haircut: 0%)
- Investment-grade corporate bonds (haircut: 8%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Hall Financial below BBB- triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
