# Credit Support Annex — Hall Financial (CP-5)

Effective date: 2026-08-16

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Hall Financial is USD 240,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Hall Financial is USD 21,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Hall Financial, with the haircuts shown:

- Cash (USD) (haircut: 0%)
- Investment-grade corporate bonds (haircut: 8%)

## Rating Triggers

- If Hall Financial's credit rating falls below BBB, the Threshold is reduced to USD 0.

## Rounding

Delivery Amounts are not rounded: the amount called is the amount calculated, to the cent, in USD.

## Settlement Timing

Eligible collateral must be transferred so that it is received by the deadline stated in the margin call notice. The deadline is set when the notice is sent.

## Dispute Resolution

If Hall Financial disputes a margin call, it must notify the Valuation Agent before the deadline stated in the notice, giving the amount disputed and the reason. The undisputed amount must still be transferred by that deadline. The parties then recalculate the disputed amount from the prices and positions at the valuation time and settle any difference.
