# Credit Support Annex — Yang Partners (CP-3)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Yang Partners is USD 90,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Yang Partners is USD 19,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Yang Partners, with the haircuts shown:

- Investment-grade corporate bonds (haircut: 8%)
- US Treasury securities (haircut: 2%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Yang Partners below BB triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
