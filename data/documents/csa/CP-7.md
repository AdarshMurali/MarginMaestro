# Credit Support Annex — Barnes Capital Management (CP-7)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Barnes Capital Management is USD 105,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Barnes Capital Management is USD 47,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Barnes Capital Management, with the haircuts shown:

- Investment-grade corporate bonds (haircut: 8%)
- Money market fund shares (haircut: 1%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Barnes Capital Management below BB triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
