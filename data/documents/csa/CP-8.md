# Credit Support Annex — Pacheco Capital (CP-8)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Pacheco Capital is USD 450,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Pacheco Capital is USD 24,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Pacheco Capital, with the haircuts shown:

- Cash (USD) (haircut: 0%)
- Money market fund shares (haircut: 1%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Pacheco Capital below BB triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
