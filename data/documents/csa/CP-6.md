# Credit Support Annex — Gonzalez Partners (CP-6)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Gonzalez Partners is USD 220,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Gonzalez Partners is USD 24,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Gonzalez Partners, with the haircuts shown:

- Cash (USD) (haircut: 0%)
- Investment-grade corporate bonds (haircut: 8%)
- US Treasury securities (haircut: 2%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Gonzalez Partners below BB triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
