# Credit Support Annex — Rodriguez Partners (CP-1)

Effective date: 2026-07-26

## Threshold

The Threshold applicable to Rodriguez Partners is USD 340,000.

## Minimum Transfer Amount

The Minimum Transfer Amount (MTA) applicable to Rodriguez Partners is USD 11,000.

## Eligible Collateral

The following collateral types are eligible for Rodriguez Partners, with the haircuts shown:

- Cash (USD) (haircut: 0%)
- Investment-grade corporate bonds (haircut: 8%)
- US Treasury securities (haircut: 2%)

## Rating Triggers

- A downgrade of Rodriguez Partners below BB triggers a review and potential increase of required collateral.
